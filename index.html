About

I'm an Aerospace Engineering undergraduate at the Indian Institute of Space Science and Technology, Thiruvananthapuram — currently in my third year.

My research interests sit at the intersection of interfacial fluid dynamics in microgravity conditions, magnetohydrodynamics for space applications, and computational plasma physics. Alongside this, I work on the development of CubeSats under the Small Spacecraft Systems and Payload Centre (SSPACE), IIST, where I'm involved in the thermal subsystem.
