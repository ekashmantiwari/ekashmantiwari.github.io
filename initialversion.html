Presenting

“Ultrasound-induced wetting dynamics of a molten metallic droplet”

International Conference on Interfacial Phenomena in Droplets

Supervisor

Prof. V S Sooraj

Dept. Aerospace Engg., IIST

Supervisor

Prof. Sathyan Subbiah

Dept. Mech. Engg., IIT Madras

The spreading of a liquid droplet results from the combined effect of capillary forces and viscous resistance. Under ultrasonic excitation, additional acoustic forcing can drive liquid motion and significantly alter the spreading dynamics. Here, the spreading and height evolution of a molten metallic droplet subjected to ultrasound of 20 kHz are investigated using high-speed imaging. The observed temporal evolution is consistent with previously reported coupling between droplet thinning, acoustic pressure, and cavitation.
